Everything You Need To Know

63° NORTH SWE CAMP is designed for self-sufficient overlanders who enjoy simple, authentic outdoor living.
Bring everything you need, leave no trace, and enjoy nature exactly as you found it.

🏕️

Camping Pitches

Spacious grass pitches suitable for 4x4 vehicles, rooftop tents and camper setups.

🔥

Fire Pit

Gather around the communal fire pit and enjoy peaceful evenings beneath the northern sky.

🪵

Firewood

Firewood is available on site for cosy campfires.

🚻

Compost Toilet

A simple compost toilet is provided to keep the camp environmentally friendly.

🧖

Sauna Tent

Relax and warm up after a day of hiking and exploring.

🏡

Community Barn

Meet fellow travellers, play darts, cook, eat and spend time together whatever the weather.

🐕

Dog Friendly

Well-behaved dogs are welcome. Please respect wildlife and other guests.

♻️

Leave No Trace

Please take all rubbish with you and help us preserve this beautiful place.
